# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard** is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed using **SQL and Microsoft Power BI**.

The project analyzes pizza sales data to understand **revenue performance, order trends, customer ordering patterns, pizza category performance, pizza size preferences, and product-level sales performance**.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful and interactive business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall pizza sales performance
- Track key sales and order KPIs
- Identify revenue trends over time
- Analyze sales by pizza category and size
- Identify top and bottom performing pizzas
- Understand ordering patterns by day of the week
- Analyze quantity sold across pizza categories and sizes
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend
Analyzes revenue performance across different months to identify sales trends and fluctuations.

### 2. Sales Distribution by Category
Shows the contribution of different pizza categories such as:

- Classic
- Supreme
- Chicken
- Veggie

### 3. Sales Distribution by Pizza Size
Analyzes sales across different pizza sizes:

- Large
- Medium
- Small
- X-Large
- XX-Large

### 4. Orders by Day of the Week
Analyzes customer ordering patterns across different days of the week.

### 5. Pizza Sales by Size and Category
Provides a detailed comparison of pizza quantities sold across sizes and categories.

### 6. Top & Bottom Performing Pizzas
Identifies pizzas with the highest and lowest performance based on revenue, orders, and quantity sold.

### 7. Revenue by Pizza Name
Provides a detailed view of revenue generated by individual pizza products.
